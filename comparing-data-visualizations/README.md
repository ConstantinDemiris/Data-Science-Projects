# Comparing Data Visualizations

This assignment uses the Pluralsight course `countries.csv` dataset to compare life expectancy in Asia and Africa.

## Requirements completed

- Loaded `countries.csv` in a Jupyter Notebook.
- Created a time-series comparison of average life expectancy for Asia and Africa, with 1952 highlighted.
- Filtered the dataset to Asia and Africa in 1952.
- Used pyplot's `hist()` and `subplot()` functions to compare the two distributions.
- Added comments and explanations throughout the notebook.

## Files

- `Comparing_Data_Visualizations.ipynb` — fully commented Jupyter Notebook.
- `countries.csv` — country, continent, life expectancy, population, and GDP dataset.

Open the notebook in Jupyter Notebook or JupyterLab and select **Run All**.
